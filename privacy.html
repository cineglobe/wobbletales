Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Wobble Tales Analytics is an internal tool used only by the Wobble Tales channel team. It signs in to the Wobble Tales YouTube channel with read-only access (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read view counts and similar statistics for our own videos, so we can make better ones.

  • It only reads data about the Wobble Tales channel's own videos. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.
  • It does not collect data about viewers. YouTube Analytics only provides aggregated statistics.
  • The data is stored privately on our own server and is never sold or shared with anyone.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Wobble Tales videos on YouTube are marked as made for kids. YouTube's own privacy policy covers viewing them.
